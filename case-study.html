Construction careers do not follow corporate trajectories. Tradespeople define their professional identity by certifications and physical on-site hours—not static PDF resumes.

The Platform

BuilderFax by Lumber

BuilderFax serves as the Apple Wallet for Workforce Identity. A free, digital credential platform designed to help construction professionals store licenses, track safety certifications, and log verified work history for instant sharing on job sites.

Free Worker Utility Enterprise Backed
Target Demographic

The Blue-Collar Workforce

Commercial wiremen, pipefitters, and solar technicians rotating across 5–10 subcontractors annually under one master trade certification. Their records live in dispatch slips and W-2s.

Core Problem SYSTEM FAILURE

Invisible Experience

The legacy BuilderFax app expected a white-collar solution in a blue-collar reality—funneling workers toward a PDF resume upload or forcing them into high-friction manual data entry on mobile keyboards. This fragmented data approach led to Workforce Fog: low contractor confidence regarding a worker's actual skills.

Relying on Paper Dispatch Slips 78%
Craft workers lacking a digital CV or LinkedIn record

System Audit & The Friction Points

The current BuilderFax app assumes a standard corporate user experience, which breaks down on an active construction site. By relying on digital resumes, broken input components, and missing system feedback, the application forces tradespeople into a high friction data entry loop.

01 / Onboarding Funnel

The Resume Dead End & Silent Failures

The Problem

The onboarding flow funnels users toward uploading a digital CV. If the PDF upload fails, the system provides zero error feedback, leaving the user completely stuck.

The Reality

Tradespeople rely on physical dispatch slips. Pushing them toward a white collar PDF flow is a mismatch, and failing silently when a document is rejected violates basic error prevention heuristics.

Heuristic #5: Error Prevention
02 / Manual Entry Form

High Cognitive Load

The Problem

The manual entry screen demands heavy typing across seven separate fields.

The Reality

Forcing a worker to type specific daily tasks into a blank description box on a mobile keyboard violates the recognition over recall usability heuristic.

Heuristic #6: Recognition Over Recall
03 / Component Ergonomics

Component Friction: The Calendar Problem

The Problem

The native date picker requires users to scroll through months and years manually.

The Reality

A standard calendar component is a nightmare for a veteran trying to log a 20 year career. It forces endless scrolling for a task that should be solved with simple duration inputs.

Decade Wheel Scrolling Fatigue
04 / Asset Organization

Rigid File Management

The Problem

Users cannot rename an uploaded image or document within the system.

The Reality

If a worker uploads a photo of a dispatch slip and the phone names it "IMG_4920", they are stuck with that generic file name. This causes organizational chaos when trying to share specific credentials with a site supervisor.

Heuristic #3: User Control & Freedom
05 / Profile Redirection

The Repetitive Entry Loop

The Problem

After enduring the manual setup, the app drops the user onto the main profile page.

The Reality

To add a second job site, the user must navigate back and restart the exact same blank form from scratch. There is no mechanism to duplicate past roles or batch multiple sites under a single trade.

System Loop Redundancy

01 / Entry Architecture

The Source Hub: Zero-Typing Ingestion

The Architectural Solution

The legacy onboarding funnel forced every user toward a fragile PDF resume upload that failed silently. The redesigned Source Hub opens up a multi-modal ingestion gateway: 1-tap Lumber ecosystem handshake, regional contractor connectors (Procore, ADP, NCCER, HCSS), camera OCR scanning for physical union tickets, and structured manual entry.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Features generous 48px+ touch targets optimized for single-handed thumb reach and dirty gloves. Eliminates onboarding abandonment by meeting tradespeople wherever their records live.

Heuristic #7: Flexibility & Efficiency of Use
02 / Pre-Sync Transparency

The Trust Handshake: Transparent Pre-Sync Preview

The Architectural Solution

Standard third-party sync modals blindly ask for account permissions without showing what data will be imported. The BuilderFax Trust Handshake searches enterprise payroll pipes and surfaces the worker's matched union local (IBEW Local 46), member ID (#784-2910), and exact logged timesheet records (1,840h at Sound Transit) before requesting authorization.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Craft workers are deeply protective of their identity data. Seeing their verified hours and union standing pre-rendered in high fidelity converts skepticism into instant adoption.

Heuristic #1: Visibility of System Status
03 / Progressive Trade Lock

Step 1: Your Role — Trade Classification & Fast-Picks

The Architectural Solution

Rather than treating every job site as an isolated form, the system decouples the master trade qualification from individual employers. Users establish their core classification once, which then anchors all past and future build sites.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Equipped with "Existing Roles in Profile (Quick Pick)" and tactile role chips (Commercial Plumber, HVAC Tech, Heavy Equipment, Framer) with intelligent trade classification auto-mapping. Replaces blank typing fields with recognition-based taps.

Heuristic #6: Recognition Over Recall
04 / Role-First Batching

Step 2: Companies Worked — Multi-Contractor Batching

The Architectural Solution

Tradespeople frequently rotate through 3 to 10 subcontractors every year under the same trade ticket. Step 2 allows users to nest multiple company cards under their locked role without ever re-entering their job title, certifications, or trade category.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Features predictive 1-tap contractor chips (+Apex, +Skanska, +Turner, +Mortenson), GPS build-site suggestions, duration selectors, and dedicated estimated hours steppers. Users can log 3 commercial stints in under 45 seconds.

Heuristic #8: Aesthetic & Minimalist Design
05 / Career Proof & Badging

Role-Wise Profile: Credential Wallet & Pay Proof

The Architectural Solution

Standard white-collar resumes treat each company as a separate entry, diluting a tradesperson's overall seniority. The Role-Wise profile rolls fragmented subcontracting stints up into consolidated trade buckets, tracking total logged hours and milestone thresholds.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Generates authoritative qualification badges like Journeyman Qualified (>2,000h Met) with visual progress indicators. Gives tradespeople undeniable proof of pay grade eligibility for union halls and general contractors.

Heuristic #2: Match Between System & Real World
06 / Continuous Audit Rail

Chronological Project Rail: Continuous History

The Architectural Solution

While role batching proves trade mastery, site superintendents frequently need chronological proof to verify recent safety compliance and active job-site tenures over the preceding 12 months.

Field Ergonomics & Site Reality

Renders a continuous vertical project rail showing sequential contractor stints, active ongoing roles, verified payroll synchronization tags, and exact project durations at a single glance.
