Section 01 / The Context

Construction careers do not follow corporate trajectories. Tradespeople define their identity by certifications and on-site hours—not digital CVs.

The Platform

BuilderFax by Lumber

BuilderFax serves as the Apple Wallet for Workforce Identity. A digital credential platform designed to help construction professionals store licenses, track safety certifications, and log verified work history for instant sharing at job site gates.

Free Worker Utility ● Enterprise Backed
Target Demographic

The Modern Tradesperson

Commercial electricians, pipefitters, and solar technicians rotating across 5–10 subcontractors annually under one trade qualification.

Core Bottleneck CRITICAL

Invisible Experience

The legacy BuilderFax app expected a white-collar solution in a blue-collar reality—funneling craft workers toward PDF resume uploads or forcing painful text input on glass keyboards. This disjointed approach created Workforce Fog: zero contractor trust in self-reported skills.

Section 02 / Heuristic Audit

The Loop of Friction: Deconstructing Component Failures

Detailed cognitive breakdown of the friction points that caused a 62% abandonment rate during first-time mobile onboarding.

01

The Resume Dead End

The primary onboarding action was "Upload your resume." This created an immediate dead end for blue-collar workers who carry physical dispatch slips, not PDF resumes. Profile automation failed on screen one.

Immediate Drop-Off
02

High Cognitive Load

The manual form demanded typing across 7 blank input fields. Forcing users to articulate daily construction tasks on a tiny glass keyboard directly violated Nielsen's recognition over recall heuristic.

7 Blank Input Fields
03

Component Failures

The UI utilized a rigid paginated year selector displaying disconnected decade blocks (e.g. 2004–2015). Forcing a 20-year veteran electrician to tap through paginated chunks caused massive fatigue and drop-off.

Paginated Date Wheel
04

The Repetitive Entry Loop

After manually finishing job site #1, the user was dumped onto a blank profile. To add gig #2, they had to navigate backward and re-type the exact same role and trade from scratch. Zero project batching existed.

No Nested Batching

Section 04 / Data Architecture

The Role-First Architecture: Select Once, Batch Many

In commercial trades, craftsmen switch contractors frequently while remaining under the exact same trade and pay grade. Our data model decouples the role from individual subcontractors.

Zero-Typing Injection

Replaced blank input boxes with tactile 1-tap contractor suggestions: +Apex, +Skanska, +Turner, and +Mortenson. Converts high cognitive strain into effortless recognition tapping.

+ Apex + Skanska + Turner

Custom Month/Year Engine

Replaced clumsy browser wheels with a dedicated iOS-style 12-month tactile grid and stepper. Added a Currently Working Here toggle that dynamically binds active roles.

Sep 2023 → Present Active

Auto-GPS & Trade Mapping

Typing "Electrician" automatically maps to "Electrical" trade without forcing users to navigate nested dropdown taxonomies. Location auto-fills major commercial metro hubs.
